# Product

**ECHO** is a browser-based cinematic narrative game and philosophical psychological thriller. Players live through short, realistic dilemmas, make three decisions per story, reach one of three endings, and read an analysis of their choices through Kant, Mill and Aristotle. Tagline: YOUR CHOICES LEAVE A TRACE.

## Users
Players who want a playable film, not a tool. They explore every story, inspect evidence, and replay for other outcomes. Desktop is the primary target; tablet and mobile get adapted, not shrunk, experiences.

## Principles
- The game never tells the player what is morally right. No good/bad colours, no score, no ranking.
- No theory is presented as the objectively correct answer.
- Evidence and memories are things the player finds and keeps (Memory Archive).
- Story content, branching logic, endings and saved progress are fixed; the redesign changes presentation only.

## Anti-references
Dashboard, chatbot window, product-card grid, generic AI landing page, neon sci-fi, cartoonish characters.
